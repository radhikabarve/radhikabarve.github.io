Business Problem

Retailers need accurate competitor pricing insights, but inconsistent product descriptions, pack sizes, discounts and multibuy promotions make direct price comparisons unreliable.

This project analyses chocolate pricing across Coles and Woolworths to identify meaningful competitive differences and prioritise products requiring investigation.

1. Recorded vs Conditional Pricing

Comparing recorded product prices with conditional Coles multibuy pricing.

2. Promotional Pricing Sensitivity

Investigating how promotions affect apparent competitor price differences.

3. Commercial Priority Robustness

Examining the stability of pricing investigation priorities under alternative scoring assumptions.

Key Business Insights

Promotional competitiveness: Conditional multibuy offers changed the cheaper retailer for 10 matched product comparisons.

Potential markdown effects: Fourteen near-double-price observations require promotional context before interpretation.

Commercial priorities: The analysis identified 27 recorded-price gap investigations and 12 conditional multibuy investigations.

Data represents a cross-sectional retail snapshot. Product matches were human-reviewed with additional SKU consistency safeguards. Multibuy prices require qualifying purchases. Priority rankings are exploratory and do not estimate sales or profitability uplift.
