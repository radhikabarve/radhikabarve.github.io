01 / The business problem

Comparing supermarket prices sounds simple. It isn't.

Two retailers can sell what appears to be the same chocolate at very different prices. But are the products equivalent? Is a promotion involved? Does the lower price require buying two? Reliable business decisions need these questions answered first.

02 / Building a fair comparison

The hard part wasn't finding prices. It was finding comparable products.

Before comparing prices, the project generated candidate matches, reviewed product identities and applied conservative eligibility and pricing-quality safeguards.
